What is Palrhea AI?

Palrhea AI is an ultra lightweight AI client designed for free, fast, and convenient local execution. Built entirely as an HTML5 ecosystem, it operates locally on your machine—meaning it requires absolutely no persistent network connectivity to run once deployed onto your device.

  • Method A (Double-Click Execution): Navigate straight into the unzipped folder asset database workspace.
